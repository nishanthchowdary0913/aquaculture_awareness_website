02 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

A community project with a clear purpose.

We bring practical aquaculture knowledge closer to farmers, aspiring farmers, young people and residents in rural communities in the Nellore region of Andhra Pradesh.

GROWWITHCARE
01

OUR PURPOSE

Better information leads to healthier fish farms.

The project raises awareness of sustainable aquaculture, encourages scientific pond management and helps connect farmers with expert advice and available support.

✳

Our approach

Listen first. Share clear guidance. Learn alongside the community.

WHAT WE WORK TOWARD

A practical path to sustainable aquaculture.

  1. 01

    Build strong foundations

    Explain pond preparation, fish seed selection, stocking and feeding.

  2. 02

    Protect fish and water

    Encourage water-quality monitoring, disease prevention and responsible waste management.

  3. 03

    Connect people with support

    Share where farmers can find Fisheries Department guidance, schemes and training.
